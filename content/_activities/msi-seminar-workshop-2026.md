# VGU Materials Science Workshop & Seminar 2026

Join us on **Thursday, 23 July 2026** for a Workshop within the framework of a ministry-level scientific and technological research project, in conjunction with the Material Science Master Program seminar.

- **13:00 – 16:00**: Seminar (Hybrid format: On-Campus & Online via Zoom)
  - **13:00 – 13:30**: Dr. Hieu Linh Duong: *Production of Syzygium nervosum Leaf Extract Nano-Formulation*
  - **13:30 – 14:00**: Dr. Thuanh Thuat Trinh: *Computational Insights into Antioxidant Mechanisms: DFT Study of Terpenoids from Syzygium nervosum*
  - **14:00 – 14:30**: Dr. Xuan Thanh Nguyen: *Computational Design for Hydrogen Storage and Purification Materials*
  - **14:30 – 14:45**: Coffee Break
  - **14:45 – 15:15**: Prof. Daniel Juhre: *Gateway to Europe: Building a Global Career in Materials Science through VGU & OVGU*
  - **15:15 – 15:45**: Dr. Georg Hasemann: *Forging the Future: High-Temperature Materials and the DAAD Scholar Journey in Germany*
