On **14 October 2026**, during the **Staff Week at the Vietnamese-German University (VGU)**, a landmark chapter in German–Vietnamese higher education was formally sealed. **Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg (OVGU)** and **VGU** officially signed and handed over the **Cooperation Agreement** for the **Master of Science in Materials Science and Innovation (MSI)** program—anchoring a bi-national engineering partnership built for decades to come.

Launched in **2024**, the Master's degree program in Materials Science has now proudly welcomed its **third intake of students** at VGU. What began as a bold shared vision between Magdeburg and Binh Duong has rapidly matured into a thriving international ecosystem of double-degree education, German flying faculty instruction, hands-on research-grade laboratories, and DAAD-funded academic mobility.

## Three Intakes Strong: From Founding Vision to Living Ecosystem

In just two years since welcoming its inaugural cohort in **2024**, the MSI program has moved far beyond blueprints into measurable student and institutional impact:

- **Three Consecutive Intakes:** With the arrival of the **2026 cohort**, three generations of aspiring materials scientists are now advancing through the MSI pipeline in Vietnam and Germany.
- **German Flying Faculty Every Semester:** Active professors and senior researchers from OVGU Magdeburg, the **Fraunhofer Institute (IMWS)**, and **TU Bergakademie Freiberg** regularly travel to VGU to teach core block courses and mentor laboratory work face to face.
- **Scholars Crossing Continents:** Pioneering MSI students from Intake 2024 have already earned competitive **DAAD Mobility Scholarships** to conduct their Master's thesis research directly at OVGU Magdeburg in Germany.
- **Industry-Connected Engineering:** Through recurring plant excursions and technical workshops across Southern Vietnam, students link classroom theory directly with real industrial manufacturing challenges.

## What This Agreement Seals: The Core Values of MSI

This Cooperation Agreement is far more than a formal document—it is an institutional guarantee of the values that define the **MSI experience** from day one of Semester 1 to Master's thesis graduation and beyond.

### 1. True German–Vietnamese Double-Degree Excellence
Graduates earn **two internationally recognized Master of Science degrees**—one from VGU and one M.Sc. from OVGU Magdeburg. Built on an interdisciplinary, prerequisite-free curriculum of **18 modules (15 compulsory and 3 electives)**, the program trains engineers to master how atomic and microstructural architecture governs macroscopic performance across metals, ceramics, polymers, composites, and computational mechanics. Explore the full academic architecture on the [Program Structure](/program-structure) page.

### 2. German Flying Faculty & Direct Research Mentorship
MSI students do not learn German engineering second-hand—they are taught directly by the researchers shaping the field. Through partnerships spanning **6 specialist Chairs at OVGU Magdeburg**, the **Fraunhofer Institute (IMWS)**, **TU Bergakademie Freiberg**, and dedicated VGU core faculty, students gain direct mentorship from professors who actively invest in student-led research ideas. Meet the teaching and research teams on [Faculty & Research Partners](/partners-lecturers).

### 3. Uncompromising Hands-On Laboratory Mastery
At MSI, theory without experimental mastery is incomplete. From the very first semester, students personally operate over **22 research-grade analytical and characterization instruments** at VGU—including **Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM)**, **X-Ray Diffraction (XRD)**, and **Thermogravimetric Analysis / Differential Scanning Calorimetry (TGA/DSC)**—with all standard machine hours and consumables covered inside tuition and **zero hidden per-sample fees**. Discover our facilities at [Laboratories & Research](/laboratories-research).

### 4. A Funded Bridge Between Vietnam and Germany
Financial barriers should never stand in the way of scientific talent. The VGU–OVGU partnership connects high-performing students with **DAAD Mobility Scholarships** for 1–2 semesters of funded Master's thesis research in Magdeburg, **DAAD Sur-Place Scholarships** for 12-month on-site support at VGU, **VGU Merit Scholarships** covering up to 100% of semester tuition, and **AFS Industry Scholarships**—supported every step of the way by the OVGU International Office. Review funding pathways on [Tuition & Scholarships](/tuition-scholarships) and prepare for your stay in Magdeburg with our [Germany Relocation Guide](/student-life/germany-relocation).

### 5. Grounded in Real-World Engineering Impact
Rather than chasing passing trends, MSI delivers the enduring foundational science that powers automotive manufacturing, aerospace alloys, renewable energy systems, sustainable construction materials, and microelectronics. Classroom mastery is reinforced continuously through industrial visits, technical workshops, and live faculty seminars documented across [Academic Activities](/academic-activities).

### 6. Inclusive Access & Modern Hybrid Flexibility
Materials Science thrives when diverse scientific minds meet. MSI welcomes graduates from Physics, Chemistry, Materials Science, Mechanical Engineering, and non-traditional disciplines such as AI, IT, Electrical, and Environmental Engineering through a supportive, consolidation-focused admission review. Beginning with **Intake 2026**, our **Flexible Hybrid Learning Format** pairs weekday evening online sessions with intensive on-campus weekend laboratories so working professionals and full-time scholars alike can excel. Learn how to join on [Future Students & Admissions](/future-students).

## Our Commitment to Future Generations of Materials Scientists

By formalizing this Cooperation Agreement during VGU Staff Week, VGU and OVGU Magdeburg commit to four enduring promises for every student who enters the MSI program:

- **Unwavering German Academic Standards:** Every module, examination, and Master's thesis defense upholds the rigor and international recognition of German university education.
- **Empowered Experimental Autonomy:** Students will always be trained as independent researchers inside real laboratories—never passive observers outsourcing their samples.
- **Dedicated Bi-National Stewardship:** Academic coordinators, research chairs, and International Office teams in both Binh Duong and Magdeburg stand side by side to guide every cohort from enrollment through thesis completion in Vietnam or Germany.
- **Global Career & Doctoral Readiness:** Whether launching R&D careers in high-tech industry across Asia and Europe or advancing into international PhD programs, MSI graduates carry a trusted bi-national credential.
