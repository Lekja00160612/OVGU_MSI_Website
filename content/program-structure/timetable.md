The MSI program employs a **Block-Modular teaching model**: visiting professors from Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg deliver concentrated lecture blocks on campus in Vietnam, complemented by hybrid weekend and evening laboratory sessions. This format provides exceptional flexibility, allowing working engineers and researchers to pursue an accredited German master degree without interrupting their careers.
