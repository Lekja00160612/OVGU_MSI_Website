The **Master's Thesis (30 ECTS)** is the flagship capstone of the MSI program — a 6-month independent, original scientific research project. Every student works under the **joint supervision of a Vietnamese faculty member at VGU and a German professor from OVGU Magdeburg**.

Top-performing candidates have the opportunity to conduct their experimental laboratory work on-site at **OVGU Magdeburg in Germany**, supported by the DAAD Mobility Scholarship (or via a self-funded research stay; see [tuition & scholarship details](/tuition-scholarships#component-2-ovgu-grant-for-masters-thesis-in-germany)).
