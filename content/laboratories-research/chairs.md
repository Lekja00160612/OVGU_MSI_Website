The MSI program is deeply integrated with **Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg (OVGU)** in Germany. Master students have the opportunity to engage in high-impact research, co-author publications, and complete their Master's Theses under direct supervision of chair-holding German professors.

Research topics span multi-scale finite element modeling, wide-bandgap semiconductors, refractory silicide alloys, and severe plastic deformation. Explore the research chairs below to learn about current projects and research directors.
