## Detailed Application Checklist

To prepare a smooth application, ensure you have the following documents ready in PDF format:

1. **Bachelor's Degree Certificate & Transcript:** Officially notarized English translation.
2. **English Language Proficiency Certificate:** IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 60+, or register for VGU's internal test.
3. **Curriculum Vitae (CV):** Highlighting academic projects, internships, and research interests.
4. **Statement of Purpose / Motivation Letter:** Especially important for interdisciplinary applicants.
5. **Passport Copy & ID Photos:** Standard administrative identification.
